A polished, riced desktop with the apps you'd expect, in one config with room to grow.

- Niri + Noctalia
- Chrome, Discord, Spotify, Steam, VSCode, OBS, Obsidian
- one theme applied to every app
- one file per feature, hosts pick the bundles they want
- LUKS, impermanence, secure boot, USBGuard and sandboxing, one line away
