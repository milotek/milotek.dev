# Don't use Kravasign
Just don't. Please.

## Why?
They switched owners, the original people seemed good, the next people were shitty, the current owners have a terrible reputation.
It might *work*, and the app default settings make it seem nonfunctional. and it might work if you're lucky, but it's slow as fuck.
All channels in their discord are read-only, where the last messages were sent a year ago, and the negative reviews were deleted from the (again, read-only!) vouch channel.

There's no point buying from them. We live in a lovely world of free markets where we can choose our service provider!

## Alternatives!
- [ArticSign](https://arcticsign.app/) seems good and cheap.
- You could also buy it from [Apple](https://developer.apple.com/programs/enroll/) themself, if you're stupid.
